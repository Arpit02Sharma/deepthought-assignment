# Daily Reflection Tree

## Objective

To help a person evaluate their day logically and improve tomorrow through structured decisions.

---

## Decision Tree

START

### 1. Did I complete my most important task today?

- YES  
   - Was it completed on time?  
      - YES → Productive Day  
      - NO → Improve time planning tomorrow

- NO  
   - Why?

      - External blocker → Ask help earlier  
      - Internal delay → Reduce distractions

---

### 2. Did I learn something today?

- YES → Record learning notes  
- NO → Schedule 30 mins learning tomorrow

---

### 3. How was my energy level?

- Good → Maintain habits  
- Low:

   - Poor sleep → Sleep earlier  
   - No exercise → Add movement  
   - Stress → Better planning

---

### 4. Did I help someone today?

- YES → Continue teamwork habit  
- NO → Help one person tomorrow

---

## Final Output

Generate tomorrow's improvement plan based on answers.
